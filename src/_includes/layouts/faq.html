Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about engagement, onboarding, responsiveness, security, and how we work with internal IT teams.

Cost & Engagement

1. How does L3 structure pricing, and how do I know costs won’t spiral?
We use a transparent and predictable pricing model based on scope, complexity, and service requirements. No hidden fees, surprise labor, or unexplained invoices. Every engagement begins with defined expectations and a clear statement of work, so you always understand what is included. As part of our Elevate approach, we align cost to maturity goals, so investments are prioritized and controlled over time.
2. Do we have to sign a long-term contract?
We offer flexible engagement options. Some services, like managed operations, benefit from term agreements for stability, while projects, assessments, and consulting can be structured as shorter commitments. Our goal is to earn trust through performance, not lock-ins. We operate with renewal transparency and meaningful business reviews so long-term decisions are based on value, not pressure.

Partnership & Transition

3. We’ve had a difficult transition before. How do you prevent disruption when onboarding?
We follow a structured onboarding process designed to stabilize first, then introduce improvements in phases. Before any transition begins, we build a transition plan that includes ownership mapping, access control, risk review, knowledge transfer, and communication cadence. We don’t rush onboarding. We protect service continuity first, then introduce enhancements as part of a planned progression.
4. We’ve had bad experiences with MSPs. What makes working with L3 different?
Many organizations tell us they’ve been disappointed by reactive service, overpromises, and poor communication in the past. We avoid that by operating with structure, accountability, and measurable results. You will always know who owns what, how priorities are decided, and what progress is being made. Our Elevate framework ensures we never default to a task-only relationship, we build maturity and value over time.

Service Delivery

5. How responsive are you—really?
Responsiveness is built into our operating model with defined SLAs, monitored support queues, and structured escalation paths. More importantly, we reduce the need for constant escalation by fixing recurring issues at the root instead of cycling tickets. You get access to a live team—no offshore handoffs, no loops of automated replies.
6. What happens if something goes wrong? Who takes responsibility?
We believe accountability is non-negotiable. If there is an outage, incident, or urgent issue, we take ownership and drive resolution. Post-event reviews are part of our process, so lessons learned translate into permanent improvements. Mistakes are addressed openly, and progress is documented.

Security & Compliance

7. How does L3 protect our environment and data?
Security is enforced in every engagement, it is not treated as an optional add-on. We apply security controls across identity, access, endpoint, network, and cloud layers as part of our baseline. We follow least-privilege access, documented change control, and secure credential handling. We also align our work with your compliance requirements, whether they're insurance-driven or regulatory.

Control & Transparency

8. Will we lose control of our IT if we work with L3?
No. You maintain ownership of your systems, data, and decisions. We document and share everything; network maps, access lists, standards, and architecture updates, so you never feel locked out of your own environment. Our goal is partnership, not dependency.

IT Team Alignment

9. Do you replace internal IT teams?
No. We support and extend them. Some clients rely on us for full service delivery, while others use us as an extension of their IT department. We can manage day-to-day operations so internal teams can focus on strategy or we can provide architecture and leadership support while your IT team handles operations. We adapt to your model.
10. How do you work with internal IT leaders and staff?
We align roles clearly from day one. Your IT team maintains full visibility and involvement in decisions. We introduce structure; project tracking, priority planning, change governance, we make sure that work flows cleanly between teams. We’re collaborative, not territorial.
